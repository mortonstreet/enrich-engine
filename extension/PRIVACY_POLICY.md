# OmniDial Chrome Extension - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 14, 2026

## Overview

The OmniDial Chrome Extension ("Extension") is developed by OmniDial ("we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Extension.

## Information We Collect

### LinkedIn Profile Data

When you visit supported LinkedIn surfaces (profile pages and list/search pages), the Extension can extract publicly visible information from the page, including:

- Name
- Job title/headline
- Company name
- Location
- LinkedIn profile URL

This data is only collected when you actively use the Extension (for example, opening the extension on a profile or manually checking selection boxes for bulk import on list/search pages).

### Authentication Data

The Extension uses your existing OmniDial session to authenticate requests. We do not collect or store your OmniDial password in the Extension. Authentication is handled through secure session cookies shared with the OmniDial web application.

### Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the Extension, such as:

- Feature usage frequency
- Error reports

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Display LinkedIn profile information within the Extension popup
- Check if a prospect already exists in your OmniDial CRM
- Enrich prospect data with contact information (phone, email) through third-party data providers
- Sync prospect data to your OmniDial CRM

## Data Sharing

### Third-Party Enrichment Providers

When you click "Enrich" on a prospect, we send limited profile data (name, company, LinkedIn URL) to third-party data enrichment providers (such as Prospeo or Forager) that your organization has configured. These providers have their own privacy policies governing how they handle this data.

### No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or prospect data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage:** The Extension stores minimal data locally in your browser (cached session info, user preferences) to improve performance. This data remains on your device.
- **Server Storage:** Prospect data you choose to save is stored on OmniDial servers as part of your CRM data, governed by the main OmniDial Privacy Policy.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

- HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
- Secure session-based authentication
- No storage of sensitive credentials in the Extension

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** View the prospect data stored in your OmniDial account
- **Delete:** Remove prospect data from your OmniDial account
- **Opt-out:** Uninstall the Extension at any time to stop all data collection

## Permissions Explained

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

| Permission                         | Why We Need It                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `storage`                          | Save your preferences and cache data locally for better performance                          |
| `activeTab`                        | Access the active tab context when you open the extension UI                                 |
| `host_permissions` (linkedin.com)  | Read supported LinkedIn pages to show profile data and user-selected bulk candidates         |
| `host_permissions` (api.omnidial.io) | Communicate with OmniDial API to enrich leads and sync CRM data                           |

## Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, where appropriate, through the Extension or email.
